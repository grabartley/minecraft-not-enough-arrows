# Ricochet arrow in flight.
#
# Rows 0-4 are the side profile, nock at x0 to tip at x15. Rows 5-9 columns 0-4
# are the fletching cross seen end on. The Textures section of the README covers
# why only that corner of the canvas is ever sampled.
#
# The item sprite's crook cannot survive the trip. It is a single hook curling
# back over one side of the shaft, and the renderer mirrors this profile onto
# itself, so a crook returns as two crooks facing each other. The glow ink sac
# hit the same wall and was moved back onto the axis; this head has to be
# rebuilt symmetrically instead.
#
# What replaces it is the one head in the mod with no shoulder. Every other
# arrow here meets its shaft at the head's widest point and tapers only forward,
# which is the shape of something that bites into a surface. This one narrows at
# both ends, a lozenge floating on the shaft, so there is nothing on it for a
# surface to catch. That is the whole read, and it is a silhouette no other
# arrow in the mod has.
#
# Warm iron rather than the explosive family's cold steel, and the tone runs the
# opposite way to that family as well. The gunpowder blade is an even grey with
# its darkest pixel at the leading edge; this head is dark at the back and
# carries its brightest face at the front, so the two separate on where the
# light sits and not only on hue. The near black outer facet is doing real work
# too: the head is warm and the shaft is warm, so without a hard edge between
# them the head dissolves into the shaft at flight size.
#
# canvas = 32
#

# m = 857C6F  hook iron, body
# d = 4A443C  hook iron, inner shadow

# S = 896727  shaft, body
# w = 5E4619  shaft, nock shadow
# F = E0E0E0  fletching, light
# f = C6C6C6  fletching, mid
